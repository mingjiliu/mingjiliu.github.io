## Your warmth flows

*2026 · Mingji Liu*

from leaf to leaf,
from paw to paw,
from cheek to cheek ...

Your smile touches
each dawn and day,
each pillow and plate,
each word and way...

Your heart bends
my core anew,
my home to you,
our lives in two.
