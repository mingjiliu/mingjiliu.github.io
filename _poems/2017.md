## 4objects 4people

*2017 · Mingji Liu*

![4objects 4people](/assets/4objects-4people.jpg)

Four individuals briefly shared a house together in Coburg North. They found each other on Facebook.

**10-sided die. Every time I roll this plastic gem, it stumbles on the table and grins at me with empty fate.**

His name was Sam. He was a tradie who regaled his housemates with his Tinder escapades. But Sam didn't actually enjoy dating women, he just kind of did it to annoy his female friends. Sam never got into any serious relationships and after a big skiing accident in August of 2015, he deleted the app and was never quite the same.

**A cleavage of rock. I found it too self-assured. But it was also reconstituted and broke, jumbled and glum.**

Greg had always wanted to learn ballet ever since he saw *Billy Elliot* one night on TV when he was little. But his father, a small town lawyer, threatened to belt him and called him a fag. Greg never moved his body to music ever again. Many years later, he refused to dance with his wife on their wedding night. He never told her why.

**The pouch. Purple. Radiant. It has a penchant for sarcasm, don't you think?**

As a teen, Evie dabbled with fortune telling, palm reading and horoscopes. After high school, she studied chemistry at UWA but dropped it halfway through. She blamed it on a bad internship at an oil refinery. One arvo, while bumming around the house, she found tarot cards in the kitchen cupboards. She stared at them for a long time.

**Matted, synthetic fur. A collection of motes and mulch. It leans over and quickly mumbles: 'ich ich ich, bitch bitch bitch'.**

Audrey was much older than the other housemates. She worked in childcare and wore heavy wool scarfs, even in summer. She owned a rescue dog and kept mainly to herself. No one knew how long she'd lived in the house for. One night, the telly airs an exposé about the earthquake in Nepal. She starts crying and stays in her room for several days.

## A vision of industry on the Werribee line

*2017 · Mingji Liu*

Never on time, has never kept pace,  
out in this hoarse and separated place,  
this sodden expanse of wedlock  
where the brownfields and cold sea meet.

Count the fingers in the tired grass:  
hard-frosted fingers flecked with glass,  
webbed with tissue paper and riddled  
with sharps among the melted sleet.

\<Train arriving. Please stand clear.\>  
\<Attention. Train arriving. Please stand clear.\>

Enter a carriage of jolted steel,  
of teething rail and grinding wheel.  
Enter and sit on a plastic seat  
kissed with weary hints of ethanol.

The station swiftly sinks from sight  
as the grey earth lurches into flight,  
leaving behind roughcast homes,  
slashed and scrawled with aerosol.

\<Attention. Train departing. Please stand clear.\>  
\<Train departing. There is nothing to fear.\>

Beyond this window, a blistered field;  
the birdless beach, scoured and peeled;  
in the distance, a nest of towers,  
an occluded and faceless refinery.

A nest of towers, enthralled in smoke,  
caged together with cauterised rope,  
given purpose by a hundred half-lit  
torches that do not face the sea.

So sit back and gaze upon the sprawl,  
watch the flames, dire and small,  
for it's hard to depart this purgatory:  
this all-consuming vision of industry.

## and his skin is the colour of God

*2017 · Mingji Liu*

Boy is GREEDY.

He snatches mayflies on the bike path,  
suspended as they are like sycamore seeds  
above the darkling grass.

Snips their paper legs,  
their abdomens of glistening treacle.  
He sews their hollow frames  
into a pendant thread of wings and eyes,  
a daisy chain of life and lies.

Boy is HUNGRY.

He harvests dead glass from the freeway.  
Beer shells. Car wire. Shreds of tyre.  
Sun-stewed upon the gutter broth.

He showers her house in detritus,  
throws carnations of wet plastic,  
burger wrap lilies and red cup roses.  
Offers a bouquet of his world,  
so blindly hurled.

Boy is DESPERATE.

His dreams are radio flash and flat batteries,  
weakly white and free-to-air.

His bed is wet, pillows clawed.  
His limbs are yearning,  
and his skin is the colour of God.

Boy is RETCHING.

If only Boy can stretch a little deeper...  
into the throat of Boy and yes!  
seize BOY and YES  
strangle BOY  
and BOY can FINALLY  
VOMIT the HEART out of BOY  
over this unwashed, ungrateful **Boy.**

Boy is PROPHET.

He feels the spine of the earth  
writhe and climax beneath his feet,  
the moan and sweat of the moon,  
the congress of stars in the evening heat.

Boy is FLOATING.

His lips are coral, his chest is thawed.  
His nails, his teeth, his genitals are gone.  
And his skin is still  
the colour of God.

## Bridge of sighs

*2017 · Mingji Liu*

When you wrote that letter of  
crippling infatuation and  
signed it off with a match, you  
stepped onto the *Ponte dei Sospiri*,  
the bridge of sighs  
all men must cross on their  
long march to life.

If only you had the courage!  
(or so you recall yourself) not to  
plunge for her with  
impassable violence and  
whirling rumination,  
gut draining the dreams from your  
marsh arab heart.

If only sibylline foresight  
could have warned you just how  
wildly your  
buttermilk love, your  
soft animal love, could  
sour into rancid despair, into  
trapped-animal desires.

But all you can do -- all any  
scorched soul can do -- is hold these  
lessons close as you  
cross over the bridge, above your  
gondoliers of mania, and  
pray that true love  
waits on the other side.

## Curios: Wolf

*2017 · Mingji Liu*

#### **ONE: **Wounded wolf. *Canis dirus.* It limps beneath the black pines, the frosted stars. Severed from the pack, it wanders and waits for the final, ragged gasps of the Pleistocene.

#### **SIX: **It tastes the sepia tones in its mouth. It smells the heaviness of its damp and rendered fur. It still feels the mastodon's tusk against its sides. Thrusting, again and again and again.

#### **SEVEN: **Wolf-hunger burns its insides. It has been unable to eat for days. The land around is as empty as the sky above --- that pitiless field of brass. It can no longer feel its extremities.

#### **ELEVEN: **The wolf imagines a bird carcass in the grass. Blanched feathers, a broken neck. It imagines a toad emerging from the bird. The wolf imagines this and moans.

## Did you ever hear the tragedy of Darth Plagueis the Wise?

*2017 · Mingji Liu*

## final Sunday

*2017 · Mingji Liu*

My mind is more than nugatory  
&                                      my aorta  
more                     than demands  
for pink fruit from our lands.

Yet strip me of such baser parts,  
like                                       a bike  
unseated              or unchained,  
what living urge is retained?

These daily defeats, humdrums,  
abiding                        apostasies,  
remake    the most lucent goals  
into saccharin for our souls.

We yearn for sterling beings,  
but    even our loftier selves  
need            succour & sleep.  
or is that too much a cheat?

I pray to enter May strong  
enough    & mind enough  
to   etch the honest tones  
of patience within my shoulder bones.
