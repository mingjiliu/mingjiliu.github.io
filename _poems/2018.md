## A Drop of Pastries

*2018 · Mingji Liu*

You did not intend to drop fresh pastries  
On the kitchen tiles---somnambulant fingers

To vice gravity. Nature obliterates that toiled  
Crust, that pregnant cream. Who betrays

Fragile sweetness or regret? Who sobs  
Into cotton mitts? The Moirai sing to slices.

An ant, that zetetic, lowliest of wayfinders,  
Is ensorcelled by full and sudden ruins:

Obelisk sugar; a scarred frieze of dough;  
Treasure made treasure from trembling fault.

Her sisters colonise the carnage---pixel  
Black trails of deconstruction. This is victory!

The birth of a new feast day! This is a dismal  
Denouement for your oven prised heart.

## An unencumbered summer morning

*2018 · Mingji Liu*

An unencumbered summer morning

on an unencumbered warming street.  
The asphalt is sun-drunk on a reservoir  
of heat. The roses waver by the fence;  
tugging at the dry humus ditch.

I see it all through the milky wedding veil  
of my bedroom curtains.

The floodlit day fades, and a tide

of cricket song rises with the half-cut  
moon. I feel sadness, too apparent  
in my lineaments. The night is so warm

from the heat rising off the asphalt  
like an invisible incandescence.

## August 30th

*2018 · Mingji Liu*

It's one of those mornings when damns punctuate  
The pee-tee-vee delay announcements  
On this drizzle footed platform.

*Damn!* this late again loitering and *damn!* my  
Trouser legs are thin; *damn* *you!* freeze assed Flemington  
And *god damn!* this wind is a grind on my cheek.

A wind so pure and pious cold  
It whips up parking lot flags; turns supermarket banners  
Into barquentine sails across the matitudinal gray.

Yet the piebald eucalypt across the rails  
Refuses to yield before the wind.

A lean trunk and chair pose outstretched;  
Its antler's crown barely shivers.  
Yes -- a flex here, a leaf chime there,  
But strangely anchored in the flow.

Instead, it reaches earthward into memories  
Of mantle green and dry rush.

Wait. Was that a spring lightness just now?  
Or do I ask too much of August?  
Ah. Patience must be bliss for  
Old gumnut trees who have lived it all before.

## Candescence

*2018 · Mingji Liu*

I did not know. *I did not know.*

How fierce the steel of our past could glow.

A past I'd buried beneath stiff dirt,

Imprisoned in stone, unmarked and inert.

Yet a trembling call, an errant encounter,  
Was all it took to stir the fire,

A pulse of desire like an organ of magma

From the heaving earth.

It rose through the dirt, a searing white coal.

It pierced straight through the crown of my soul.

. . . . .

No one dares hold a sword lashed in flames.

No hand could plunge that furnace of pain.

Yet I forgot that even removed,

Steel from that forge can take years to cool,

And all that time, suppressed in the black,

It's heart was candescent with a longing

That always came back. *It always comes back.*

## Exegesis on the Wasp

*2018 · Mingji Liu*

Whistling across sear fields of anointed conjecture,

Some scholars speculate that the infernal fruit,

Viper-plump and Eden-bane, was no rosy apple

But in fact grape, citron, or pomegranate

(Some claim psilocybin, but those

Sages are admittedly orthogonal).

For what it's worth, I blame the fig,

*Ficus carica* with her lapsarian buds of sweetness.

For Adam & Eve were vegans in their wild nudity.

Lambs napped with lions and there was whole grain for all.

They stood *primus inter pares* among the flesh of creation,

Too busy naming all under heaven to wonder  
What they tasted like, you see.

Their veganity was their godliness.

Put fig and man together, and you get *Homo plummetus*.

The taste of fig stripped them of their vegan ways ---

For fig is wasp flesh. It is the

Succulent sugars of dead insects.

A fig orchard is a holocaust of anthropoids.

Nature's a fucking bastard, I tell you.

The fig wasp is truly a demented machine --

The female, heavy with eggs, squeezes

Her way into the yonis of the nascent fruit,

Mutilating herself as she crawls.

Wingless, antennaeless, good mama

Lays her eggs -- male and female both.

Once hatched, the brothers and sisters

Fornicate with each other like Hapsburg royalty.

All this carnal action fertilizes the fruit,

Then the pregnant daughters leave Gomorroah

To continue the cycle of incest,

While men and mother die and are absorbed

By the fruit in a chemical soup.

Point is, we should rename it an Adam's fig and

Next time you bite into one,

Remember that it is sweet, juicy

Inbred insect orgy murder sauce.

## Fairy lights

*2018 · Mingji Liu*

My housemate, before she left

Hung fairy lights on the passion fruit

Vines. Solar powered, they blinked

On every night since. She is gone

(Melbourne was too cold, she said)

But the lights, which reminded her

Of the fire flies on the Mae Klong

Remain. That's the thing about

Us, us who stay and us who go.

Off, it all bull rushes back to us

Like the phosphorous heart beat

Of insects in the dark.

## Fun Darwin trivia

*2018 · Mingji Liu*

Contrary to popular belief, the famous British naturalist Charles Darwin never visited the city. Instead, Darwin was founded in 1869 by his cousin, Chuck Darwin, an overweight beagle breeder who discovered the harbour after making a 2,100 nautical mile wrong turn at the Strait of Malacca.

For a brief period, between 1990-1994, the Northern Territory legislature was suspended and replaced by a large boab tree (*Adansonia gregorii*), making the Territory the first ever dendrocracy in recorded history. 

You may be familiar with 'The NT News' headlines (e.g. "Horny roo stalks NT women"). But did you know that no human actually writes them? The headlines are, in fact, the product of a malfunctioning mechanical haiku generator -- "Basho Gone Troppo" as it's affectionately called by locals.

If you squint really hard, you can actually see Indonesia on the horizon.

When walking through the George Brown Darwin Botanic Gardens, you may notice signs warning you not to pick up chunks of asbestos on the ground. This is because asbestos is used as a nesting material by the rare and endangered Australian white ibis, who require it for successful egg incubation.

In July of 2017, in an innovative attempt to stimulate the slumping Darwin property market, Chief Minister Michael Gunner offered free Royal Australian Air Force (RAAF) F/A-18 Hornets for first-time homebuyers.

Common wifi passwords in Darwin: box_jellies, buffalo3, croc0dile, salt!water, kAkadu

## Kamakura study trip

By the time we arrived in Kamakura by minibus,

I was a foul, pauper's bed mood.

I, a whimpering fool begging in your dorm.

I raged at lilypads, who did not care.

The lantern wall rolled their eyes.

When the gay American tried to lecture me

On how to purify my hands from the temple trough,

I snapped. My god.

I thought about cheating on you in Miyajima.

Curse the iron Buddha in his thronehold,

All the buddhas, all screwed.

My brain half-screwed, full of holes.

Everyone gave me such a wide berth.

They ate sweet potato ice cream instead

And sipped the 'Pocari Sweat'.

Last straw of the giant's shoes.

A sign at the lookout said simply,

'Beware of Kite'

'They'll aim for your food, fly from behind.'

'Their sharp claws will injure you.'

'Please be careful!'

What right did I have to be so angry?

My trip to Japan in

2011

Ruined me. I cannot hide this

Anymore.

## moon of moon

*2018 · Mingji Liu*

I read in *New Scientist *that if a moon is full enough  
estranged enough from its host planet,  
she can catch her own moon in the celestial dancehall of things;  
think about that! servant for a servant, pet of a pet,  
a pendant strung around another  
---at least, that's what the uranologists theorise  
(having never spyglassed such phenomena in reality).  
'moonmoon' they proclaimed this maybe moon,  
to the Internet's fleeting delight.  
they're tireless minds, these earth-class astrogators,  
spitballing a cosmic bestiary of  
nomad worlds and strange stars  
a million, billion light beats away.

that same week, a biopsy came out from the IPCC:  
it said we're converting this cradle into a crucible.  
it carved into the sarcoma of human industry  
and charted a course away from end times.  
yet the voyage fee was swiftly scorned---  
'We admire the Carboniferous too much',  
opined Canberra. 'We'll take our chances with smokestacks.'  
I read that in a single generation  
there might be dengue in Townsville,  
a Great Barrier Undercroft,  
and everyone settling Hobart, eating bread from Russia.  
already, my friend laments on Twitter that it's  
twenty-five C in London

in mid-October. this world isn't real anymore.

I wake up at two AM in naked sweats. I gather my skin for a rinse  
but the showerhead has a weak pulse.  
I peer out the frosted casement, and remember  
that the metropole blinds all possibility  
for stargazing in a vinous canopy of light.  
a half-thought lurches: if other earths exist, do they too  
speculate on supernal maybes in the stellar ink?  
do they too wonder  
on stray and feverish nights,  
whether they'll live to cash out their mathematics?  
to confirm with their own oracular eyes  
a moon embracing another?

## Parallel

*2018 · Mingji Liu*

There's been a snarl in the clime,  
the warp dissolving into weft,

a lolling November ambushed by hail burst  
in this young, unbalanced city.

They say that's just Melbourne,

confluence incarnate,

just another torrid affair that

barely entertains the afternoon.

Yet everyone's still stunned by the

glass-hearted towers together.

## Pillow stone

*2018 · Mingji Liu*

When it came my turn in the circle  
To hold Julia's newborn, my nerves betrayed roughness.  
No, I didn't drop the babe---are you kidding?*  
*But when I tried to nest her softness against my chest,  
My graceless motions could not soothe.  
Her pinkness whimpered into crimson, and  
Mother promptly made a repossession.  
(Like half-broken horses, I think they know  
A nervous rider by heartbeat.)

Dismay coursed within. The shame of  
Lacking a pastel touch and soft-hue limbs;

Synapses unable to balance

## Pumpkin 1

*2018 · Mingji Liu*

In the pantry, the pumpkin rots. A corruption seeps  
through abandoned space.  
The autumn globe is scarred by a green-blue  
crater, ringed by shockwaves of hairy, cataract  
white. Some mindless force has turned neglect into moist and  
unventilated life.

Yet this genesis is no miracle.  
This waste is no miracle.

I write from my office, as I do when I try to impress  
or burn time with women.  
I compose a floweret of plastic words and phrases,  
too easily plucked from that crooked larder,  
from that *spence --- scullery --- store --- *in my skull.  
A gift built on the fallacy of spontaneous life.

Don't look or touch or taste.  
Just more sad waste.

This gift was first made in a childhood shed, a dim  
sepulchral tin, where rags and  
mushrooms grew. Hansons Lane, I think,  
back in the bad old days. Or maybe Blair Ave,  
a cold house with crooked fire bricks and moaning cats.  
Rough place number four.

'What are you doing over the weekend?'  
she asks politely.

'Remembering my childhood  
and struggling to throw old vegetables out.'

## You can see it from the Moon

*2018 · Mingji Liu*

They say you can see it from the Moon,  
That middle kingdom firewall

With red ramparts and watchtower tall,

A chain of censors burning perfumed smoke  
Around one-third of Homo digitalis.

But lo! Look how the muddy grass horses

Graze brazenly beneath its purview;

Watch how the river crabs endure --- *O Pooh!*

It's wise not roffle at their enclosure,

For we too are as a cage full of birds.

Turn your ISP west and know these chilling words:

Every stroke of datum's logged and keyed  
In prismatic, ice-bound servers in Fort Meade.

We're trapped with the power out

In the worst snow storm in living memory,

With God's five eyes upon us.

We were promised freedom in the hyperweb,  
Burning chrome, the decentralised sprawl;

Not the balkanised net of a digital thrall,  
Dogged by backdoor codes and Ivan bots

Let slip from the vale of silica.

(Good name for a band: *Anna Lytica*)

Such is the twist to Panopticon,

With Nothing-To-Hide, Done-Nothing-Wrong.

Should we smash the screen? Off the line?

Wear tinfoil hats and go clandestine?

If only it were so easy to execute.

Your cyber-soul might be sold and damned,

Yet still you wonder whether they'll

Like your most recent on Instagram...
