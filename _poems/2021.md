## "The Stranger"

*2021 · Mingji Liu*

Every morning, the sunlight  
Arrives like a stranger in your foyer.

A soft ray, a harsh glare,

A momentary flush of gold

Through the cold glass of the door.

Watch as it lifts the dust

From the slumbering hardwood,

Marvel as it sheds off the skin

Of yesterday

Like an old overcoat.

The new day, the known and the not known,

You must greet this light and invite it  
In for breakfast.

Open yourself to this daily stranger,

Because they are here to

Create your life anew.

## Commissioner Lin interrupts my spliff

*2021 · Mingji Liu*

I was puffing under porch lights, admiring the stained gables and ivy touched  
railings and my graceless attempts at kneading dope with shag  
  
when a face dappled up the inky growth. I rose, unsteadily, to greet my interloper,  
but before I could clear my throat, the man was on the creaking boards.  
  
His eyes were fierce beneath his cap, silk tasselled and bauble topped,  
with a peacock feather set in its slope. He pointed a pelted sleeve at me  
  
while his necklace, punctuated by corals and berry black cabochons, rattled dice like  
from his motions. Tall, aggravated, adjudging, he spoke from lips half hidden  
  
by whiskers about diseased men and foreign predation. He spoke of hard choices,  
diplomats, a riverbank. 'But the Manchus are dead, or in glass jars,' I replied,  
  
'your station was lost after the Gardens burned. Crawl back into old books, old man.'  
He winced at my odour. He pursed as if to say *this one does not appreciate*.  
  
'I come from below a courtly stature also,' he said. 'I come from watching ships and  
sailors burn at bay, from watching towns wheeze on the promise of a pipe.  
  
I know craving is a mute plague and that to cut the individual bleeds the collective.'  
'And don't assume I'm oblivious to measures,' I retorted.  
  
'I remember climbing the stairs to report sharps in the yard. I know of weights,  
coverings, hysterics. I know my limits and my cadences.  
  
My body is sovereign and without legacy.' To which, he jeered:  
'Boy, you've choked your mind. Your reason is chained, not captained by choice.  
  
You perfume consumption after the fact, knowing full well the conflicts in your bones.'  
'Damn you, old fool! Textbooks can rot, and grievances can smoulder  
  
a century more for all I care! How can you bind narratives  
with what little I smoke in seclusion?' But to no response --  
  
he had dissolved, stilling the animus on my porch. The night was mothy.  
I stood, blood eyed, as a breeze took away the last of my ash stock.

## consort.

*2021 · Mingji Liu*

lament, artemisia, lament!  
rake grief across seedless fields,  
pour salt into swollen hills,  
scream yourself  
into village wells from here to  
lycia to phygia to pisidia to  
OH GOD NO --

lament, sister wife, lament!  
smear my ashes black to broken lips,  
dissolve my body in trembling wine

and drink within the mausoleum

of our linens,

serve your subjects

this throat tearing thirst,  
courtiers eunuchs dancers emissaries  
make them taste what it's like  
to lose a season entire,  
make them all know  
PLEASE NO --

and know that  
no matter how grand  
a vow was spoken,  
no matter how high  
the marble has risen,

I will never return. not ever.

## Flowers

*2021 · Mingji Liu*

It doesn't matter  
if these are happy  
or unhappy flowers.  
These are stockyard  
blooms, monstrously  
plump from sepal  
to anther, gorged  
with colours and  
grown to be cut.

Penned in  
glass gardens,  
windless and  
seasonless, fed  
with lamp lights  
until they reach their  
spotless, perfect  
place between  
the blades.

The butchers lace  
their bodies with silk.  
Hecatomb petals.  
Idol cones of nectar.  
Let your lovers roll  
in these perfumed  
beheadings.

The underworld  
must be infinitely floral.  
Rivers of blossoms,  
caverns filled with them.  
Unbrowning, unbruising,  
choking in bouquets.

## Jul Aug Sep poems

*2021 · Mingji Liu*

i

Fatigued, in slumber...  
Like potatoes in wet earth...  
I become my own couch,

Cushion tuber, living room root,  
Snacking upon myself...  
Feeding, waiting,  
  
Wondering when the  
Tiding sun, the hinting spring,  
And asperges of sweet rain,  
Will give me leaves, flowers

More than roots!  
Will give me space like  
a dandelion's space,  
To float off, finally, with the wind.

ii

in-grown cheese & wet sock salad

fungal flaked with fresh heel callus

sweats & nails & sweetly scabrous

chow this down, you nummy palate.

iii

the leaves, descend,  
falling like ash from  
the orange-cold hearth.  
they are the dust of seclusion,  
a sign that the eyes are closing  
for another year.

iv

The dawn, cat-faced, gingerly  
glints through the curtains,  
purrs into the stainless basin,  
and licks at my unwashed cups.

Unloved, or not, she's a pet that's always here,  
and a life that never stays.

v

There's a stranger's tear in my eye,  
a meeting in the arcade, after dark,  
the tiles like prison bars, the brass  
clock in the hanging post, its arms  
flung between terrifying before and  
the beautiful beyond, clamps down,  
vices, and embraces me.

vi

Crushed,  
by the stones of sleepless nights and bootheels,  
by a chest pressure,  
by diving, cranking, torquing scrapyard force,  
  
Crushed,  
by thumbs and screw-presses and lead weights,  
by voices and fault finding and regrets and grief  
  
Like blind sea animals in a prehistoric ocean,  
squeezed through aeons, mineralised and imprisoned,  
crushed by rocks into rocks themselves

Crushed,  
the fragrance of sesame seeds, crushed, till every drop of  
oil is gone, the tartness of grapes, crushed till every dribble  
of juice is in the bowl  
  
Crushed  
into fragrance, into sound, into memory,  
like a god or a curse.

vii

Spring's dark clouds ---

Lilting pressure ---

A wheel spins in the child's hand

viii  
  
Paddock glazed with frost,

Goats bleat for milk,

She opens the gate before sunrise.

ix

man, thrice-godded,  
divine and desert,  
child of sand and rock,  
brings the key to the hole.

battery low,  
our stories tell us nothing,  
lines on the walls, scratches,  
what questions are these  
  
in this time without answers?

xi

Tranced by the pink horizon,

Like a hen by lines

In its sand.

## Life model 

*2021 · Mingji Liu*

I tried to approach her gingerly at first,  
but my pencil snagged on the hips  
--- too angular, too small, too exacting in my nib.

So I took orchestral strokes,  
pouring charcoal into her breasts.  
No good. by the time I reached the neck  
I was lost in fatty rubbings. 

And nothing frustrates like the face,  
The nose a cipher, lips illegible,  
and those eyes ---  
I'd meet those eyes in repose  
and shy away. so dangerously nice.

Still, it's better than trying to pluck  
the essence of a plum or a cold-lit vase.  
Squeezing awkward lines into awry limbs,  
muttering my fingers with  
a chalky, scratching idiolect of my own. 

After class, the model approaches,

*how did you go?* she says. *can I see?*  
I shuffle my pages and cannot bear to show  
her my translations of humanity.  
Again, I shy away.

## Poem

*2021 · Mingji Liu*

*For Grace*

We spent the whole afternoon,

flinging paper planes

as hard as we could.

We wanted to test

the range and the limits

of everything we possessed.

You tossed your frisbee

down the cul-de-sac,

again, and again,

while I pitched my kite

above the pylons

before snipping the line.

In the end,

we took the family bird

and prised open her cage.

We watched her perch

on the railing, ungainly at first,

before catching the wind

and vanishing into dusk.

I'm sure she's still out there,

scouting the whole world for us,

tracing longitude and latitude

in spaces we cannot.

Soaring higher than peaks

and dearer than freedom,

gliding over the map

and brushing its edge.

She's probably still rising,

close to the stars, rising

to the place where celestial bodies are.

## Standing

*2021 · Mingji Liu*

I'm waiting for dawn to grasp with candy-coloured fingers,

waiting for a call about my licence registration,

waiting for them to reopen the FoodWorks and the Freechoice,

waiting for the next election between Broad-Gauge and Vanilla-Cream,

waiting for the poplars to turn gold, then green, then gold again,

for friends to pick their weddings and divorces,

for the return of Halley's Comet,

for ice caps to become distant memories in nursing homes,

for the erosion of the Pyramids at Giza,

for the continents to fuse into a new Pangaea,

I'm waiting for the Second Coming, for the Age of Krishna, for the death of ignorance, the end of darkness and decision,

for the sun, having engulfed her children, to menopause into a perfect white dwarf,

I'm waiting for colliding galaxies and melancholic black holes,

for the last proton, that quivering dancer, to dissolve into epitaph.

They ask me why I'm standing here ---

what there is to see atop the pedestrian bridge down by the tenements ---

not much, I try to assure them, and yet everything at the same time.
