## Nikki

*2025 · Mingji Liu*

you are the quartz in the sand, its glow along the strand;

> you are the hours on the beach, the warmth of your reach,

you are the views from a plane, of the bell towers of Spain;

you are the flow of her streets, its contours and its beats,

you are the green in my tea -- green as ferns, and as trees;

you are the spices in my bowl, a heart that is whole.

## North Queensland

*2025 · Mingji Liu*

**i. CASSSAURIUS**

from out the bush

coarse and black,

She lifts her crown to collect  
sunlight leaking through leaves

rainforest monarch,

ruby laced and blue

She stamps a three-knifed foot  
on the berry laden floor

undaunted and deliberate

striding back into her forest.

**ii. PSEUDOCARANX**

Silver that I hunger  
Silver that I circle  
Vast silver eyes  
Soundless listless sands  
Under the silver hull  
Between the silver lights  
Along these currents I silver  
The half-moon I silver  
Corals -- a ghost town  
You -- interloper  
In your silver tank  
In my empty  
Silver night

**iii. RHINELLA MARINA**  

Glory be to me!  
Tonight,  
I gave a kiss to thee.

These tricks I do for free!  
Despite,  
The warts and all I be.  
  
I'm here for good, you see?  
All right.  
`Tis time to race me.

## Strelitzia

*2025 · Mingji Liu*

Rain, fitful and wind-cast,  
spoils spring  
for days.  
A lean bird-head shivers  
greenly  
by your window.

Then sleepily, cautiously,

an inch of blue

emerges.

She reveals a gold-tint

tuft.

Then another.

Till one day, you wake  
to her daring

plumage.

A wingspan, paradisical,

ready

to embrace you.
