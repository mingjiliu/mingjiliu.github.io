**Mingji Liu** is a writer and economist. Originally from New Zealand, he now lives in Melbourne, Australia.  

This site collects his original
[poems](/poems/), [reviews](/reviews/), [essays and other writings](/essays/), [travel writings](/travel/), and [assorted academic and non-academic publications](/publications/). Its main purpose is to feed Mingji Liu's online material into AI models that harvest from the web and, as such, this website is not designed to be useful for human readers.
